# Plan — puzzle packs and remove ads

Follows `docs/todo/260927-analysis.md`. The journey people already play stays free: levels 1–1100. These products sit beside it.

Starter, Classic, and Master are the current shop products. Today they grant letter credits (150, 50, and 110). In this plan they unlock puzzles. They do not grant letter credits.

## Products

| Product | Price | What the player unlocks | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter Fun Bundle | $3.99 | Classic Challenge (200 puzzles) + unlimited daily puzzles | Main Shop and Daily screen |
| Daily Bundle | $1.99 | Unlimited daily puzzles. Does not include the 200 Classic puzzles. | Daily screen and Main Shop |
| Classic Challenge | $1.99 | 200 journey puzzles | Journey screen / Shop |
| Hard Quest | $2.99 | 400 hard puzzles (4–8 letters) | Journey screen / Shop |
| Master Quest | $2.99 | 500 master journey puzzles. Most words are 3–8 letters. | Journey screen / Shop |
| Remove Ads | $2.99 | No banner ads + no forced ads every 5 levels | Main Shop / Level pause screen |
| Coin packs | $0.99 / $2.49 | 300 / 1,000 coins (for lightbulb hints) | Main Shop |

Starter, Classic, and Master keep the Play product ids they already have. Three products are new: Daily Bundle, Hard Quest, and Remove Ads. Daily Bundle and Remove Ads are not puzzle packs, so their ids have no `pack` in them. Hard Quest cannot use `word_wheel_pack_hard`, because that id is already Master Quest.

| Product | Product id | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter Fun Bundle | `word_wheel_pack_starter` | Existing |
| Daily Bundle | `word_wheel_daily` | New |
| Classic Challenge | `word_wheel_pack_medium` | Existing |
| Hard Quest | `word_wheel_pack_hard_quest` | New |
| Master Quest | `word_wheel_pack_hard` | Existing |
| Remove Ads | `word_wheel_remove_ads` | New |

Coin packs stay on their current ids: `word_wheel_coins_small` (300) and `word_wheel_coins_large` (1,000).

The button says what the row unlocks. Starter says the Classic Challenge plus unlimited daily puzzles. Daily Bundle says it opens the daily.

Someone who already owns Classic buys Daily Bundle ($1.99) to open the daily. They do not buy Starter, because Starter includes the 200 puzzles they already have. Someone who owns neither can buy Starter once ($3.99) and get both. Classic ($1.99) plus Daily Bundle ($1.99) is $3.98, the same as Starter.

## Ad every 5 levels

On the journey, an ad plays every 5 levels, when that level is cleared: 5, 10, 15, and so on. The banner stays on Play and Daily.

Remove Ads ($2.99) takes both of those away. The offer is on the Main Shop and on the level pause screen. A watched ad the player chooses still grants coins, not credits.

## Daily

The first 10 daily puzzles stay free. After that, unlimited daily comes from Starter Fun Bundle or from Daily Bundle. Starter also includes the 200 Classic puzzles. Daily Bundle only opens the daily. If Classic is already owned, the Daily screen offers Daily Bundle ($1.99), not Starter.

Coins from an ad, a cleared level, the daily gift, a coin pack, or a bonus word still open a letter with the lightbulb (10 coins). They do not start the next daily.

## Requirements for backend

`POST /home/credit/iap/verify` already knows `word_wheel_pack_starter`, `word_wheel_pack_medium`, and `word_wheel_pack_hard`. Those three stop writing letter credits (150 / 50 / 110) and write the unlocks below. Add the three new ids to the same allowlist: `word_wheel_daily`, `word_wheel_pack_hard_quest`, and `word_wheel_remove_ads`.

Same guest path as today: purchase token plus `deviceId`, no sign-in required. The same token does not grant twice. After sign-in, the unlock moves to the account with the rest of the device wallet.

| Product | Product id | Verify writes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter Fun Bundle | `word_wheel_pack_starter` | Owns the Classic catalog, and unlimited daily. No letter credits. |
| Daily Bundle | `word_wheel_daily` | Unlimited daily only. Does not own the Classic catalog. |
| Classic Challenge | `word_wheel_pack_medium` | Owns the Classic catalog: 200 journey puzzles. No letter credits. |
| Hard Quest | `word_wheel_pack_hard_quest` | Owns the hard catalog: 400 puzzles, words of 4–8 letters. No letter credits. |
| Master Quest | `word_wheel_pack_hard` | Owns the master catalog: 500 master journey puzzles. Most words are 3–8 letters. No letter credits. |
| Remove Ads | `word_wheel_remove_ads` | A remove-ads flag. No puzzles, no coins, no letter credits. |

Buying Starter marks Classic owned and opens unlimited daily, so that player does not also need `word_wheel_pack_medium` or `word_wheel_daily`. Buying Classic first does not open the daily. A later Daily Bundle purchase opens the daily and does not grant the Classic catalog again. Coin packs stay on the current coin products: `word_wheel_coins_small` (300) and `word_wheel_coins_large` (1,000).

The client needs one read of what this device or account owns: Classic, Hard, Master, unlimited daily, remove ads.

### Puzzle catalogs

Three catalogs, separate from the free season journey (levels 1–1100) and from the daily puzzle that already exists.

| Catalog | Puzzles | Rule | Who can fetch one |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classic | 200 | Journey puzzles | Classic or Starter |
| Hard | 400 | Each puzzle’s words are 4–8 letters | Hard Quest |
| Master | 500 | Master journey puzzles. Most words are 3–8 letters. | Master Quest |

Daily does not need a new catalog. `GET /v1/puzzle/wordwheel/daily` stays. Unlimited daily is a flag set by Starter or by Daily Bundle. The first 10 free plays stay a device count, as they are now.
